      About Restore

      A small, quiet tool.

      Restore cleans isolated vocals with a fixed sequence of local audio models and carefully limited processing. Your recordings stay on this computer.

      Choose a mono or stereo recording between 1 second and 30 minutes. Keep Restore running while it works; closing this tab is fine. Models can take several minutes, especially on a CPU.

      Use Before / Cleaned to switch at the same playback position. WAV is 24-bit; MP3 is 320 kbps.

      The studio profile uses your licensed Nectar, Waves X-Click and soothe2 plugins. The optional portable profile has different processing and is experimental. Neither profile guarantees removal of every artifact.

      Settings live in config.json. Restart after editing. Completed recordings and saved stages live in output/jobs, or your configured output folder.

      The configured Sucial breath model is licensed for non-commercial use. See the model terms before using it for paid or monetized work.
